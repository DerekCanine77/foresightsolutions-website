Illustration

From an exception to a decision

This is an illustration of how Foresight structures the work. It is not a production product, and it is not a system that has been built in Foundry.

Detect the exception

Name the item that is stuck. It might be a quality hold, a margin change, a missed qualification, or another break in the work.

Investigate the evidence

Look at the source the decision depends on. Note what is missing, conflicting, or too old to trust.

Challenge and validate

Test the rule that is blocking the item. Separate a real constraint from a check that no longer matches the work.

Human approval

A person other than the reviewer decides whether the item can move. The illustration keeps review and approval apart.

Governed action and audit

Record what changed, who approved it, and what still needs to be watched. The record is part of the decision, not an afterthought.
