How engagements are structured

Each engagement is structured around one operating constraint.

Foresight is a specialized technology and execution firm. An engagement is scoped to one constraint: a trusted data path, Ontology design, an operational application, AIP readiness, adoption, governance, or how a decision gets made. When the team already runs Palantir Foundry, the advice covers how that platform should carry the decision. The work is designed to leave clearer ownership and a repeatable path when the engagement ends.

One constraint

Shape the engagement around the operating bottleneck leadership needs removed.

A decision in view

Anchor the work to a workflow, a domain, an AIP use case, or another bottleneck that affects the operating cadence.

One path

Coordinate data, shared definitions, the application, the workflow, adoption, and governance as one engagement.

What remains

The engagement is designed to improve patterns, documentation, ownership, and handoff quality that stay after the work ends.

Fewer single points of failure

Make progress less dependent on a few people by clarifying standards, decisions, and repeatable ways of working.

Easier next use case

Each engagement is designed to make the next use case easier to launch, not only to clear the current backlog.
