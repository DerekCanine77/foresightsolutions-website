Privacy

Privacy notice for Foresight Solutions LLC.

This page explains information you choose to send, the contact form, and a traffic script the hosting provider may add on its own. Foresight Solutions does not add an analytics product to this site today.

Information you provide

Foresight Solutions may receive contact details, company information, role information, project context, and AI scale diagnostic intake details when you email the firm or submit the form. A scheduling link is not published today. If one is added later, booking details would be included here.

How information may be used

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, prepare for conversations, evaluate fit, improve the intake process, and maintain business records related to prospective or active engagements.

Hosting provider script

The site is hosted by GoDaddy. GoDaddy may inject its own traffic and performance script, served from img1.wsimg.com (including tccl.min.js), on live pages. Foresight Solutions does not include that script in the site files. The script can send technical and usage data to GoDaddy, including to csp.secureserver.net, under GoDaddy’s own policies. Foresight Solutions does not control the script. The hosting account owner can ask GoDaddy to disable it.

Analytics Foresight does not add today

Foresight Solutions does not currently add Google Analytics, a LinkedIn Insight Tag, or another analytics tag. Google Search Console may be used later to read search performance; it does not by itself place a tracker on the page. If an analytics or advertising tag is added, this notice will be updated before it goes live. Those tools can collect technical, interaction, referral, or conversion data under their own policies.

Form submissions

Formspree processes information submitted through the diagnostic form and delivers it to Foresight Solutions LLC. The form asks Formspree to send you to the thank-you page after a successful submission. That redirect depends on the Formspree plan or dashboard settings. Do not submit confidential, regulated, or sensitive business information through the form unless an appropriate agreement is in place.

Scheduling data

No scheduling link is published. If a scheduling link or embed is added later, scheduling details may be processed by the selected provider, including name, email, meeting time, and notes provided during booking.

Contact
