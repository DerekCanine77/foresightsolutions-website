Focus areas

Operating problems concentrate in a few kinds of work.

These are focus areas, not a record of completed engagements. They name the environments Foresight is built to work in, including healthcare technology. Manufacturing, supply chain, industrial operations, data platforms, and enterprise AI programs are focus areas in the same sense.

Manufacturing

Typical problem: A quality, maintenance, or throughput exception is visible, and the decision still sits outside the flow of work.

  • Cross-plant Ontology object types and reuse
  • Quality, traceability, maintenance, and throughput workflows
  • Where a Workshop view would need to sit near a frontline decision
  • AIP use cases that would need process and asset context

Healthcare technology

Typical problem: Operational data has to serve several groups, and it is unclear who owns the decision.

  • Operational data products across complex stakeholder groups
  • Governed AI readiness for sensitive workflow environments
  • Clear ownership between technology, operations, and domain leaders
  • Adoption design that respects clinical and operational realities

Supply chain and logistics

Typical problem: An inventory, supplier, or fulfillment exception is visible, and the next action is not.

  • Exception management, inventory, supplier, and fulfillment workflows
  • Regional variation without fragmented Ontology object types
  • Decision paths that connect visibility to action
  • Advice on where AI could support prioritization or routing

Industrial operations

Typical problem: An asset, reliability, or work-order event does not reach the person who has to decide.

  • Asset, reliability, field execution, and work order contexts
  • Data paths that would need to support operational confidence
  • Shared patterns for physical assets, events, and Actions
  • How distributed teams would use the same decision path

Data platform organizations

Typical problem: The platform team and the operating team do not share one definition of a decision-ready dataset.

  • Governance, release, and reuse standards
  • A clearer bridge between platform teams and operating teams
  • How domain-facing applications should be scoped
  • Where leadership can see the bottleneck

Enterprise AI programs

Typical problem: A pilot showed promise, and the operating decision it was meant to change is still manual.

  • Use case readiness and prioritization
  • Controls for workflow-connected AI
  • Operating ownership for AI-enabled decisions
  • A path from a pilot to a repeatable decision
Domain lens

The right operating model respects how work actually moves.

Manufacturing, healthcare technology, supply chain, and other operational environments need different workflows, controls, and adoption paths. Foresight is built to align platform capability to those realities. A focus area is not a claim that an engagement has already been completed there.

AI becomes useful when it understands the work, not only the data.
