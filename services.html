Engagement models

Three ways Foresight engages.

Foresight engages in three ways: Palantir specialist staffing, a statement of work, or managed services. Foresight is independent of Palantir.

Model 1

Palantir Specialist Staffing

Foresight sources and places specialists with Palantir Foundry, AIP, and data-platform skills. The client directs the specialist's day-to-day work. There are three ways to place a specialist.

Contract

Fits when the client needs a specialist on its team for a defined term.

What it is
The specialist works on the client's team for a defined term.
Who directs the work
The client directs the day-to-day work.
How it is billed
Billed for time worked.

Contract-to-hire

Fits when the client wants to work with the specialist before deciding on employment.

What it is
The assignment starts as a contract. The client has the option to convert the specialist to its employee after an agreed period. Conversion terms are agreed up front.
Who directs the work
The client directs the day-to-day work during the contract. After conversion, the person is the client's employee.
How it is billed
Billed for time worked during the contract. Conversion terms are agreed up front.

Direct hire

Fits when the client wants a permanent role on its own payroll.

What it is
Foresight recruits for a permanent role on the client's payroll.
Who directs the work
The client directs the work as the employer from the date of hire.
How it is billed
A one-time placement fee when the person is hired.
Model 2

Statement of Work

Foresight delivers a defined scope. The scope includes agreed deliverables, milestones, acceptance criteria, and a timeline. Foresight owns delivery. The client accepts the deliverables against those criteria.

Fits when the outcome, the deliverables, and the timeline can be named before the work starts.

Who owns delivery
Foresight.
Who directs the work
Foresight directs delivery against the agreed scope.
How it is billed
Priced fixed or by milestone, as agreed.
Model 3

Managed Services

Foresight provides ongoing support, operation, and enhancement of an agreed scope. The scope can include pipelines, Ontology objects, or applications. Foresight owns that work for the term of the engagement. Service levels and reporting are agreed for that engagement. They are not published as standard figures.

Fits when a defined scope needs an owner on a continuing basis.

Who owns delivery
Foresight.
Who directs the work
Foresight directs support, operation, and enhancement of the agreed scope.
How it is billed
A recurring fee.
